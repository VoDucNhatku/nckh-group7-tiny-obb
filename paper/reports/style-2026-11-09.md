Checklist of the `style-humanizer` skill, applied to the rewrite of `paper/main.tex`. This is a heuristic check; it does not
claim that any AI detector would score the text in a particular way.

- Semicolons: 45 → 19 in the main text; the remaining ones separate list items (the four rules, the outcome classes).
- Flagged terms (delve, crucial, robust, comprehensive, notably, moreover, ...): none found by the scan.
